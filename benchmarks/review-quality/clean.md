# Observational study of participation

## Methods

We observe 120 people, of whom 60 participate in a voluntary program.
Participation was not randomized. The estimates describe associations.

## Results

There are 60 participants and 60 nonparticipants in the analysis.
Participants have a mean outcome 0.5 units higher than nonparticipants.
This association does not establish that the program caused the difference.
